## Mono-color Prompt

Art direction only; image generation was unavailable and this prompt was not executed.

Wide, flat editorial journal cover on neutral white #FAFAF7. Two ink plates: charcoal #30343A for a mechanically screened mountain landscape and reading text, signal red #C83232 for small editorial markers. No third ink.

One continuous landscape field with an open left reading area and a mountain ridge rising toward the right. Relaxed composition, about 45 percent exposed paper. No framed photo panel or split-card layout.

Preserve the mountain ridge, valley and forest of the source photograph. Use fine variable-size halftone dots, exposed-paper highlights and a controlled fade into the left margin. No color wash or photographic blur.

Overlay the exact live title "朝向自然" in a Chinese editorial serif, with the existing sentence "人生，本就是一场无限的自我探索游戏。" below it. Small utility labels use a neutral sans or monospaced face. Never bake essential text into the image.

Clean contemporary printing, restrained dot texture, no artificial aging, beige tint, gradients, ornaments, distressed borders, shadows, cards or invented publication marks.
